## Writer

- Style: name the instruments of a full band or production and words like breakdown, build-up, drop.

## Arranger

- Plan an energy curve with one deep valley: the sections before the breakdown at energy 3-4.
- The breakdown is the bridge or the section before the last chorus (in an instrumental the section before the last one or two): energy 1, lead "none" or "pad" - only chords, room to breathe.
- The section after it builds again (energy 3, arpeggio); the final section is the fullest (energy 5, riff or countermelody).
- The breakdown may borrow a darker or suspended chord; the key stays.
