## Writer

- Style: name the instrument that plays the signature figure (for example a bell, a piano, a whistle synth, a violin) first.

## Arranger

- Invent one memorable figure of one or two bars (3 to 6 notes, a clear rhythm) and give exactly the same figure in every section with lead "motif".
- Use it in the intro, in every chorus and in the outro; Plenio moves it onto each bar's chord, so it sounds familiar but follows the harmony.
- Between its appearances keep the line simple (pad or arpeggio) or silent, so the figure stands out when it returns.
- Energy: verses 2-3, choruses 4, the final chorus 5; the key stays.
