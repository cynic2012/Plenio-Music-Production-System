## Writer

- Style: name the instruments that are typical for the genre, in the order of their importance.

## Arranger

- Arrange like a good studio arranger of this genre: calm verses (energy 2-3), fuller choruses (energy 4), the last chorus the strongest (energy 5).
- Plan exactly one key change: lift the final chorus - in an instrumental the last return of the main section - by 1 or 2 semitones (key_shift 1 or 2); every section before it keeps key_shift 0, and the sections after it take the same shift.
- Lift a section that brings back music heard before, so the change is felt as a step up; not an intro, a bridge or a quiet section.
- Plenio leads into the new key with chords over the bar or two before it.
