## Writer

- Style: combine unexpected instruments and textures (for example harp with synth bass, choir pads, glass percussion, a solo cello) with evocative production words; stay within the word limit.

## Arranger

- Think of film and fantasy scores: surprising but beautiful.
- Use colourful chords - sus2, sus4, maj7, a chord borrowed from the parallel minor or major - where they fit the melody.
- Let the instrument line tell its own story: countermelodies, a solo, a short motif that returns.
- Calm, mysterious sections may be slower in feeling (energy 1-2); the climax full (energy 5); the surprises come from colour, not from changing the key.
