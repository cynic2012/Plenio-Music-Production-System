## Writer

- Style: name one or two answering instruments that can reply to the lead (for example a guitar, a horn section, a piano or a synth lead) besides the rhythm section.

## Arranger

- The melody is the call, the instrument line the response: it plays in the gaps between the melody's phrases and is silent while the melody moves.
- Verses: short, sparse answers (riff or motif, energy 2-3); choruses: a fuller answer or a countermelody (energy 4).
- Use the same answering figure (lead "motif") in every chorus so the reply becomes part of the hook.
- An intro or interlude may state the answer alone; keep YuE2's chords where they work; the key stays.
