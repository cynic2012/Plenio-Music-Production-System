## 1.3.1 - 2026-10-27

**The player on every screen, and the studio started from it.** The Plenio Player is laid out for the phone, the tablet and the large screen - a tab bar and a slim player you swipe on a phone, sheets instead of long pages, a rail through thousands of songs - and stays quick with thousands of songs. Help and the video tutorials are inside it. Songs are made from the player alone: presets set the whole form (*Best quality* with HQ mastering and cover art, *Party - fast* …), *How many* orders up to four takes or songs at once, and the player starts ComfyUI by itself when a song is ordered - no workflow to open. At the party the guests' reactions float up the big screen and *Up next* names the song that follows. The Song API 1.1 adds downloads, waiting and filters, with a guide for other programs; every template has a note on which model file goes where.

- **Phones and tablets**: a tab bar and a slim player to swipe on a phone, filters and menus as sheets, Now playing that fits, *Add to Home screen*; the slim player and a drawer on a tablet. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#on-a-phone-a-tablet-and-a-large-screen)
- **Help & tutorials** in the player (**H**): guides by topic with a search, and the tutorials playing in the page. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#help-and-tutorials)
- **Presets and takes**: *Best quality*, *Party - fast*, *Quick sketch*, *Full song*, *Three takes*, *Instrumental* and your own; up to four takes of a song or four songs in one go. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#party-songs-guests-make-songs)
- **The studio from the player**: ComfyUI started and stopped in the background - by hand, or by itself when a song is ordered and after idle minutes. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#the-studio-comfyui-started-by-the-player)
- **Reactions and Up next**: 🔥 ❤️ 🎉 👏 💃 😂 from the guests' phones float over the big screen, Live Play counts them per song; a card names the next song in a song's last seconds. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#guests-in-your-network)
- **Thousands of songs**: the library compressed (9.6 MB -> 1.0 MB with 2,958 songs), small covers, the folders read in the background, a search keystroke in 61-81 ms instead of 125-147 ms.
- **Song API 1.1** with a guide, every field, examples and an OpenAPI reference for other programs. [Details](docs/api/README.md)
- **Models · what goes where** in every template: the files, the folders as a tree and what to choose for each graphics card.

### Added

- **The Song API for other programs: a guide, every field, examples and a reference to browse.** `docs/api/README.md` takes a programmer from the first request to the finished files - quick start in curl, Python and JavaScript, how a song is made (the job's states as a diagram, the stages), every endpoint, the request in detail, following a job, the files, covers, a new take from a release record, every error code with what to do, retries, access from other computers and from web pages, versions and limits. `docs/api/fields.md` lists every field of both templates with its type, default, values and when it applies; `docs/api/examples/` has ready-to-run scripts (Python with the standard library only, Node.js 18+, curl and jq); `docs/api/openapi.json` is the OpenAPI document as a running ComfyUI gives it, browsable on the project's site (`/api/`). The copies are made from the shipped templates by `tools/api_docs.py`, and a test keeps them current.
- **Song API 1.1** (only additions - a program written for 1.0 works unchanged): **a finished song's files to download** - every file of a job's `result.files` has its `url` (`GET /jobs/{id}/files/{name}`, byte ranges work): the audio, the unmastered take, the cover, the sheet music, the subtitles and the release record, only what the record lists; **waiting instead of asking every second** - `GET /jobs/{id}?wait=30` answers as soon as the job's status, place in the queue or stage changes (at most 60 s); **the list filtered** - `GET /jobs?status=queued,running&client=My%20app&template=yue2-song&limit=50`. The OpenAPI document names every call (`operationId`, for client generators), describes every answer (the job, its stage, progress, result and files, the queue, a template's fields) and has examples.
- **The Plenio Player on a phone and a tablet.** A phone gets a tab bar at the bottom (*Songs*, *Playlists*, *Live*, *Make* or *Help*, *More*) and a slim player above it - swipe it sideways for the next or the previous song, up for Now playing; swipe Now playing down to close it. The library begins with the song that plays, your collections as chips and the songs right below them - on an iPhone 13 the first song now shows at 412 px instead of 1398 px, below the fold before -; the filters open as a sheet from the bottom, a song's menu and Now playing's ⋯ too, and a tap on a row plays the song. Now playing fits the phone (the lyrics were cut off at the right). A tablet gets the slim player and the menu as a drawer. *Add to Home screen* opens the player like an app, with its own icon, on the whole screen.
- **Help and the video tutorials in the player.** *Help & tutorials* (in the menu, or **H**) has short guides by topic - first steps, finding songs, Now playing, the party, making songs, phones and other computers, videos and shared players, what to check when something does not work - with a search, and the narrated tutorials play in the page (from youtube-nocookie.com, and only after a click: nothing is loaded from YouTube before).
- **Presets for making a song.** *Best quality* (HQ mastering, cover art, the best of three takes for the words, FLAC and MP3), *Party - fast*, *Quick sketch*, *Full song*, *Three takes* and *Instrumental* set the form - and with the whole studio its settings and takes - in one click; **Save as preset…** keeps every field of the form, the studio's settings and the takes under a name of your own. A preset sets only what applies: the party's guests get its length and vocals.
- **Several takes or several songs in one go** (the whole studio): *How many* 1-4 - takes of one song (made, then sung and played anew from the same words and notes, at the head of the line) or different songs of one form.
- **The studio from the player: ComfyUI started without opening it.** *Settings › The studio* says whether ComfyUI runs, starts it in the background (its own Python, the folder and arguments you set, a log), stops a ComfyUI the player started, can start it by itself when a song is ordered and stop it after idle minutes; *Make a song* offers *Start the studio* when it does not run. The player plays without ComfyUI; ComfyUI is needed only to make songs.
- **Reactions from the party's phones.** The guests' page has 🔥 ❤️ 🎉 👏 💃 😂 under the song that plays; a tap floats up an edge of the big screen - over the library, Live Play, Now playing and the visual on the whole screen -, Live Play and the phones show how the room reacted to each song. At most 60 a minute from one phone and 40 on the screen at once; *Reactions* in the guests' settings switches them off.
- **Up next** on the big screen: in a song's last 15 seconds a card names the song that follows, with its cover.
- **A rail beside long lists** jumps anywhere: sorted by title its letters, by date its months (years when there are many), by length its minutes, by tempo its tens - click a mark or drag a finger along it.
- **Settings in sections**: chips at the top jump to *Accounts*, *In your network*, *Party songs*, *The studio*, *Sound* … instead of a long scroll; links such as *Switch it on in Settings* open the right section.
- **A note in every template: which model file goes where.** Like ComfyUI's own templates, each template opens with *Models · what goes where* beside *About this template*: the files it needs and the optional ones, each with its link, size and licence, the folders as a tree under `ComfyUI/models` (with the lyrics ASR Plenio fetches itself), the writer models of your own (GGUF, LM Studio, Ollama …), and for every size of graphics card - below 8 GB to 24 GB and more, and without one - what to choose, with the files for a smaller or a larger card. *0 · System Check* shows the whole set of every template. The notes are made from the model catalogue, so they and the loaders' downloads always agree; the view opens with them in sight.

### Changed

- **The Plenio Player with thousands of songs** (measured with a library of 2958 songs): the library goes compressed to a browser that takes it (9.6 MB -> 1.0 MB) and is answered *unchanged* to a page that has it already (`304`, no body) - a phone in the network loaded all of it at every start; the cards get small covers (a 256-pixel picture: 12 KB instead of a 207 KB cover), made once by Pillow into the player's cache; the page's own scripts go compressed too. Reading the folders again no longer holds up any request: a page, a song's page or the audio that streams get the songs as last read at once, while the folders are read again in the background - before, every request after 15 seconds waited for all records to be read, under one lock, and Live Play asked every two seconds; the folders are walked in one pass that leaves out the folders the library skips (0.34 -> 0.14 s for 44,000 files), the cache of 12 MB is written only when a song came, went or changed, and numbering the titles that repeat no longer takes a time that grows with the square of the library (0.7 s of every reading). While the folders are read the first time, the page hears how far it is.
- **The player's search on a large library**: a keystroke takes 61-81 ms instead of 125-147 ms with 2,969 songs (the search waits for a pause in the typing and keeps each song's folded text).
- The templates' *About* notes name the new *Models* note instead of a short list of their own.

### Fixed

- **The player's page could be shown inside another site's frame**, where a click meant for that site could land on the player's *Remove…*: it now refuses every frame but its own (`X-Frame-Options`, `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors`), and every answer says `nosniff`.
- **A form of another site could make the player read every record again** (*Look for new songs*, `POST /api/rescan`): only the player's own page can now.
- **A request that failed inside the player closed the connection without an answer** - the page saw a network error; it now answers `500` with the reason, and the player's log keeps the details. A request with a wrong `Content-Length` is refused instead of failing.
- **Two requests with one Idempotency-Key at the same time could make two songs** in the Song API; they now make one.
- The Song API's field `source.audio` (a cover's recording) described itself with nothing; it now says how to upload a recording and give its name.
- Now playing on a phone was wider than the screen: the lyrics were cut off at the right and the section names ran over.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-10-26

**Party songs, a Song API, and videos that change with the song.** The listeners of the Plenio Player make songs of their own: a style, a few choices, *Generate Song* - and when the song is ready it plays in Live Play. Any song of the library is made again from the player - a new take of it, a new song like it or a cover of it -, and whoever opens the player from the network has the whole studio: every setting of the template and of Export Release. Behind it the new Song API on the running ComfyUI makes a song from a template's fields with one HTTP request, for any program, and follows the template when it changes. A video's visuals now follow a plan over the song - one visual, a new one at every section or every few seconds, each chosen in a gallery of moving previews playing to the song's own music - with new looks. And the Song Brief has *sparse vocals*: a song that is mostly instrumental, with a few sung phrases.

- **Party songs**: *Make a song* in the player and *Make your own song* on the guests' phone page - a new song or a cover of an uploaded recording; a vibe, a style template or *Surprise me* fills every field; *Your songs* shows each song's place, a rough time it will be ready and what the studio does now. Off by default: the admin decides who may, how many songs each and how long - and whether the name of the one who made it shows while the song plays. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#party-songs-guests-make-songs)
- **Again, from any song**: a new take - the same title, words and notes, sung and played anew -, a new song like it or a cover of it, from a song's page, its menu, a playlist or Live Play. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#again-a-new-take-a-new-song-like-it-a-cover-of-it)
- **The whole studio from your network**: whoever opens the player through *In your network* chooses the writer model, HQ mastering, cover art, the rest of the brief, the folder, the formats and the tags - at any time, without the party's limits. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#the-whole-studio-from-your-network)
- **The Song API** (`/plenio/api/v1`): a template's fields read from the template and the node definitions on every request, values checked with the field and the reason named, jobs followed through queue, stage and result, uploads, cancel, idempotency keys and an OpenAPI description; a song's release record turned back into its request, for a new take of it. [Guide](docs/user/concepts/song-api.md)
- **A video's visuals over its time**: per section or span a chosen visual or one at random from a seed, the same visual for every chorus, changes on the beat; the cover behind the visual, the beat's breath, grain, vignette, cinema bars, section names, an end card and fades from and to black. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#save-a-song-as-a-video)
- **Sparse vocals**: a few hook lines among instrumental sections, for YuE2 and MiniMax Music 3.
- **Filters**: several values in one row of the player's filters - *jazz* and *pop* keep the songs of either.
- **Lyrics fidelity** renders a further take only while the best take so far sang less than 96 % of its lyrics.

### Added

- **Party songs: guests and users make songs from the Plenio Player.** *Make a song* in the sidebar (and *Make your own song* on the party guests' phone page): a new song or a cover of an uploaded recording; a style - a vibe (*Pop*, *Rock*, *Latin* … - each click another template of it), any style template, or *Surprise me* - that fills every field and the description; genre, mood, tempo, length, vocals, language, voice, theme, key and meter as lists of every value the templates have, each with a value of one's own; **Generate Song**. *Your songs* shows each song in its order - its title once made, before its style, length and voice -, in line with its place and a rough time it will be ready, being made with how long and what the studio does now (*writing the lyrics*, *composing*, *singing and playing* …), or ready with ▶; a waiting song moves past one's next song, is changed or taken out, one being made is stopped. The admin decides in *Settings > Party songs* whether logged-in users and guests may (off by default), how many songs one person may have in the making, the longest length and whether covers may be made. Every party song is made by YuE2 with HQ mastering off and cover art on, in `plenio/Party-Songs-<date>`, album *Party-Song <date>*, artist the user's name or *Guest*; when ready it joins Live Play as every new song does (the player reads its folder also when it is not one of its folders). The songs wait in the player's own line and go to ComfyUI one at a time; the estimates follow how long the songs before took on this computer (measured on an RTX 5060 Ti: a song of about 1:00 in 2 to 7 minutes, the first after a start the longest). Nothing of it in a shared player (`plenio.player.maker`; the guests' page is a small page of its own, `plenio/player/guest_static`).
- **Who made a party song, while it plays** (the party sees whose song it is). *Settings > Party songs > Who made it*: *spotlight* shows the name of the one who made it - the user's name or *Guest* - large as the song begins (letter by letter), with every new section and as it ends, a small badge in a free corner between; *drift* lets the name float slowly across the picture between those moments. In Now playing and over the visual, glowing with the beat; the party's phones show *made by …* under the song that plays and in *Up next*. Not shown until the admin chooses; the name comes from the song's tags (album *Party-Song <date>*, its artist), so songs made before show it too (`plenio.player.maker.party_maker`, `MakerCredit.vue`).
- **Again, from any song of the library: a new take, a new song like it, a cover of it.** *A new version…* (the wand) on a song's page, in its ⋯ menu, in a playlist's rows and in Live Play's *Up next* and *Played*, and *Again…* on a finished song in *Your songs*. **A new take** keeps the song's title, style, lyrics, score and cover prompt as its release record has them - the Song Sheets keep them as manual, so the writer and the plan do not run - and draws only a new take seed; the title, the style and the lyrics can be changed first (new words get new notes; a cover's words stay, they follow the original's melody). A take skips the writing and the planning; its render and its export take as long as a new song's of its length (on an RTX 5060 Ti 2:44 for a take of a 1:30 song, the models loaded). A take of a cover also reads its original's melody and words again. **A new song like it** fills the form from the song's brief, **a cover of it** takes the song's own audio file as the original - uploaded to ComfyUI, the library is not touched. A choice that is not possible says why: a new take is YuE2's and needs the record's lyrics and score, a cover's also its original recording in ComfyUI's input folder. Each of the 2926 YuE2 release records of Plenio 0.1 to 1.2 on a test machine gives a valid take request. The party's guests make again the songs they made themselves; a song made again never takes over the collision rule of the one it comes from, so it gets a new number instead of overwriting it (`plenio.player.maker.PartySongs.remake`, `MakeForm.vue`).
- **The whole studio from your network.** Whoever opens the player through *In your network* - and the admin - finds *Every setting of the studio* under the simple form: the writer model, HQ mastering, Cover Art, the rest of the Song Brief (arrangement, genre closeness, creativity, lyrics fidelity, a cover's excerpt) and Export Release - the folder below ComfyUI's output folder (made when it is not there), the name and collision rule, FLAC, MP3 and WAV, the tags, the sheet music and the subtitles. The fields come from the Song API's description of the template, so a field a later version adds shows by itself; a field shows only while it applies (a voice for sung vocals, the tag fields while tags are written), and one line says how the song will be made. From the network songs are made at any time - also while song making is off for the party - and without the party's limits; *Settings > Party songs > From the network* switches it off (`StudioPanel.vue`, `PartySongs.full`).
- **The Song API: songs made with one HTTP request** (`/plenio/api/v1` on the running ComfyUI) - for the player and for any other program. `GET /templates/{id}` lists a template's fields - every input of its song groups (*1 · SONG*; the cover's *1 · SOURCE* and *2 · COVER*: the brief's fields, `writer.model`, `finish.hq_mastering`, `finish.cover_art`, `source.audio`, the optional excerpt), read from the template and the running ComfyUI's node definitions every time, so a field a later version adds is in the API at once -, the Export Release's fields and the seeds; `POST /jobs` checks the values against the node definitions (a refusal names the field and why), writes them into the workflow, draws the seeds as the template's *new seed* switch does and queues the run through ComfyUI's own `/prompt`, with the workflow in the record (*Open in ComfyUI* works for it); `GET /jobs/{id}` follows it - queued with its place, running with the stage, succeeded with the record and files, or failed with the node -, `DELETE` cancels; `POST /uploads` takes a cover's recording; an `Idempotency-Key` makes a repeated request one job; `GET /openapi.json` describes it all. The workflow is turned into ComfyUI's API prompt by Plenio itself (subgraphs, promoted widgets, bypassed and muted nodes, DynamicCombo inputs), checked against the prompts ComfyUI's frontend makes of both templates. Review stops are not supported yet; the API is ready for them ([guide](docs/user/concepts/song-api.md), `plenio.core.song_api`, `plenio.core.workflow_prompt`).
- **The Song API makes a song again**: `documents` in a job request go into the Song Sheets as manual, so the run keeps them (a document the template does not keep is refused by name), and `POST /plenio/api/v1/from-record` turns a release record into the request it stands for - the template, the fields, the release fields, the seeds and the documents -, with whether its documents make a new take here and why not, and the values the template no longer takes in `warnings`. Sent without `seeds.take` it makes a new take of the song, without documents and seeds a new song of the same brief ([guide](docs/user/concepts/song-api.md#again-a-new-take-a-new-song-of-the-same-brief)).
- **A video's visuals planned over its time**: one visual all the way, a new one at every section or every few seconds - and for every section or span the visual chosen in a **gallery of little windows, each playing the visual to the music of that part**, or one at random (from the visuals ticked in the visuals' settings, those that suit the song, or every visual; *Shuffle the random ones* draws others; the preview, the strip and the video show the same). The same visual for every chorus and every verse, a change on the beat, other colours at every change; the choices are kept per song.
- **More looks for a video**: the cover behind the visual (blurred, drifting, glowing through its dark), the picture breathing with the beat, how strongly the visuals react, film grain, darker corners, cinema bars, the section's name as it begins, an end card with the cover, the title and who made it, and in from black and out to black.
- **Sparse vocals: a song that is mostly instrumental, with only a few sung phrases.** The Song Brief's *vocals* has a third choice between *sung* and *instrumental*: *sparse vocals* keeps language, voice and theme, and the writer writes only a few short lines that fit the song and carry its melody - a hook phrase, a call, a short chant or wordless sounds (*ooh*, *hey*). It gets a section form of its own: two to four *[Hook]* sections (about one a minute) with one or two lines each among empty *[Instrumental]* sections and a *[Break]* before the last hook - as many sections as a sung song for YuE2 (which plays the empty sections as instrumental parts), as many as an instrumental for MiniMax Music 3 (which ends short maps early), whose caption says that the voice sings only now and then. At most two lines per hook: 6 for 3:00, where a sung YuE2 song has about 22 and a MiniMax one 36. With a score of its own (the DAW template) the score's sections stay and the words go into one to three of them. The draft and the Song Sheet warn when the lyrics have more than twice as many lines, and MiniMax's check for too few lines leaves such a song alone. Templates take `vocals: sparse vocals`; the player's *Vocals* filter has *sparse* (`vocals:sparse`). How the music models plan and sing such lyrics has not been measured yet (`core.lyrics.sparse_form`, `sparse_line_limit`).

### Changed

- **Lyrics fidelity renders a further take only when it is needed** (runs with *high* or *highest* no longer take twice as long for a take that already sings its lyrics). *high* renders the second take only when the first sang less than 96 % of its lyrics' words as written, *highest* the second and the third the same way: a take of 96 % is close enough, and the run goes on with it at once. Before, *high* always rendered the second take and *highest* a third when neither reached 95 % (`core.fidelity.TARGET`).
- **A video fades in from black and out to black** by default (*The look*; switch it off for the old cut).
- **Several values in one filter row of the player**: the chips of one row are alternatives - *jazz* and *pop* keep the songs of either genre, two tempo ranges both tempos -, the rows must all match, and a chip that leaves songs out always does. Tempo, length and creativity, one choice at a time before, take several too; only the Plenio version stays one choice (*from 0.8.0* already holds every newer version). A row counts without its own chips, so its other values say how many songs they add. Two chips of one row, which kept only the songs with both before, now keep the songs with either - saved filters too.
