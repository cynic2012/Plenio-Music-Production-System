## 0.8.1 - 2026-10-15

The **Plenio Player**: your songs in the browser, without ComfyUI - lyrics lit up as they are sung, the sheet music following the music, playlists and Winamp-style visuals. Lyrics subtitles for every song, timed by listening; tags filled from the brief with variables; seeds with one plain switch; wider briefs and short tooltips beside the node.

### Added

- **Plenio Player** (owner's request): a local website for the songs you made - `python plenio_player.py` (or `python -m plenio.player`) with ComfyUI's Python opens it at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/`, ComfyUI need not run. It reads every release record below ComfyUI's output folder (`--folder` for others); playlists, favourites, play counts and settings live in `~/.plenio/player`.
  - **Library**: a featured song, search over titles, lyrics, genres, moods and briefs with field filters (`genre:jazz`, `bpm:>110`, `key:Am`, `model:yue2`, `lang:german`, `is:fav`, `has:pdf`, `"phrases"`, `-word` ...), filter chips for the common genres, moods, models, languages and creative modes, eight sorts, grid or list; songs without a cover get artwork from their colours.
  - **A song's page**: it follows the music (the next song's page on the same tab, Back unchanged); the lyrics (click a line to hear it, the line sung now stays in view), the notation following the music (click a note to jump), the sheet music PDF, every detail (the brief, model and writer, loudness, the seeds with a copy button, the tags, the cover's prompt), all files to download, the song's other takes and songs alike.
  - **Now playing**: Stage, Lyrics (karaoke, syllable by syllable), Notation (the notes sounding light up, the sheet scrolls along) and Visual (the visual full screen, the lyrics as subtitles, the controls fading away); the sections as chips to jump to; the waveform as the seek bar with the sections and, under the mouse, the line sung there; a title card per song. Crossfades between songs, **A/B** with the unmastered take (in step, no gap), speed 0.75-1.25× keeping the pitch, resume where you stopped, the system's media keys.
  - **Visuals** after Winamp, MilkDrop and AVS: 13 visuals (Classic Bars, Oscilloscope, Stereo Vectorscope, Radial Bloom, Feedback Tunnel, Hyperspace, Plasma, Inferno, Kaleidoscope, Beat Fountain, Spectrum Ridges, Ripples, Retro Grid) drawn from the spectrum, the waveform, the stereo image and the beats. Modes: *match the music* (by genre, mood and tempo), *random* from the visuals you tick, *cycle*, *fixed*; a visual of its own per song and per playlist (the song's wins, then the playlist's); a change with every song, at every section, every few seconds or when the music drops in; five colour looks and a sensitivity.
  - **Remove a song** (owner's request): 👎 *Remove…* on its page or in its menu lists the song's files and moves them to the retired songs (`_retired` in the library folder, which the player does not read; move them back to have the song again) or deletes them for good after asking once more. The song leaves the queue, the playlists and the favourites; only the files in the song's own folder are touched.
  - **Playlists and the queue**: smart lists (favourites, most played, recently played, never played), your own playlists with drag to reorder, a colour and a visual each, a radio of songs alike from any song, the queue to reorder or save as a playlist, and a sleep timer that fades the music out.
  - Keys for everything (Space, arrows, F, V, N, L, S, R, B, M, Q, /, 1-4, ? for the list). Guide: [Plenio Player](docs/user/concepts/player.md).
- **The lyrics' times in the release record**: Export Release also writes the measured times into the record (`timing`: per take the lines, each syllable's start and its place in the score, the warp against the score), so the player shows lyrics and sheet in step without reading the SRT files.

- **Lyrics subtitles** (owner's request): Export Release writes the lyrics next to the song as `<name> (lines).srt` - one subtitle per line, from its first sung word to its last - and `<name> (notes).srt` - the same lines painted on note by note, a new subtitle at every syllable (YuE2, on its note of the score) or word (MiniMax). The times are **measured**: the lyrics ASR (faster-whisper large-v3, fetched on first use) listens to the exported song, its words are matched to the final lyrics, and with a score the score is fitted to them (a time warp per song, a shift per line), so the syllables keep the score's rhythm; where score and ear disagree, the ear wins. A YuE2 render is shifted against its score - 1.7 s in the median song, up to 15 s - so the score alone would be no subtitle. Measured on 48 of the owner's YuE2 songs against a forced aligner: half the words within 0.16 s, 82 % within 0.5 s (Whisper's own times: 66 %). Without lyrics (*Enhance & Master*) the subtitles are the words as heard; instrumentals get none; offline without the model the export goes on and says why. No SRT styling tags (YouTube and video editors read them differently). New toggle **lyrics subtitles (SRT)**, on by default; the record lists the files.
- **Tags with variables** (owner's request): every tag field and the file name pattern take `{genre}`, `{mood}`, `{language}`, `{title}`, `{lyrics}`, `{model}`, `{writer}`, `{take_seed}`, `{date}`, `{plenio_version}` ... (43 variables: the song, the brief, the run, the models, the other tags), `[optional {parts}]` that vanish when a variable is empty, `{{ }}` for literal braces. A typo stops the run when it is queued, before anything renders. Export Release's new **brief** input gives the brief's fields; the templates connect it and write `{date}`, `{genre}`, `{lyrics}` and the comment `[Genre: {genre} · ][Mood: {mood} · ][Language: {language} · ]Powered by Plenio …`, and a **Tags & variables** note beside Export lists every variable. Every tag field also takes a connected text output.
- **A lyrics tag**: the *tags* option's new field *lyrics* (FLAC `LYRICS`, MP3 `USLT`; kept when the cover is embedded), `{lyrics}` in the templates.
- **The briefs' fields as outputs** (owner's request): Song Brief has *template, genre, mood, tempo, length, vocals, language, voice, theme, key, meter, arrangement, genre closeness* and *lines under the singing* as outputs of their own (the brief's final values, the template's where a field is empty); Cover Brief its twelve. Appended after the old outputs, so saved links keep their places.
- **Plenio · Seed** (owner's request: seeds everyone understands): one switch, *each run* **🎲 new seed** or **🔒 same seed**. The new seed is drawn **before** the run, so the number shown is always the one the last run used - switch to 🔒 to keep what you just heard (ComfyUI's *control after generate* shows the next run's number). Amber renews, blue keeps; a line under the switch says what it means for its part, and **🎲 new seed now** draws one number at once. The templates' Take seed (🎲), Draft seed (🔒) and Arrangement seed (🔒) are Plenio seeds, with their switch in App mode. Older workflows keep ComfyUI's Seed nodes; continuing a record made before 0.6.0 puts its seeds on the new nodes.
- The brief's panel says what the chosen **creative mode** does (*Creative mode hypnotic: A steady repeating pattern …*), and so does the brief's summary - your own mode files' descriptions too.

### Changed

- **Wider briefs** (owner's report: the inputs' text was cut): Song Brief and Cover Brief 520 px instead of 380; the templates moved to make room.
- **Tooltips beside the node, and short** (owner's report): ComfyUI draws a tooltip above the mouse, over the node's own fields - the tooltip of *genre closeness* covered the fields above it, and the creative modes' list (2,425 characters) ran off the window. The tooltips of Plenio nodes now stand beside the node at the hovered row (on the left when the right has no room); ComfyUI's own placement stays for every other node. No Plenio tooltip is longer than 220 characters (17 were): the details moved to the help pages - the Song Brief's lists every creative mode.
- An Export Release saved in *tags* mode by an older version gets an empty lyrics tag after its composer, so its values stay on their fields.

### Fixed

- **The last line of a node's summary was hidden**: the run summary under a Plenio node (the System Check report, Export Release's files, Apply Arrangement's harmony check ...) asked the frontend for the height of its text only, but the frontend lays a DOM widget out with a margin of 10 px above and below - so a summary sized to its text stayed 20 px short and cut off its last line, however tall the node was made (found while recording the tutorials again). The summary now counts the margin too.
- The file name pattern's `{seed}` was always empty (the export did not know the seed); it is the take seed now.

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-15

The song's FLAC carries its whole project: drop it onto ComfyUI - or open it in *Continue a Plenio song* - and you are back in the workflow it was made with. A free cover gets a new melody over the writer's chords: below *song flow closeness* 80 the melody follows the new harmony - from 50 the notes that clash move, below 50 Plenio writes the melody anew in its old rhythm. The writer sees every note of a bar, the harmony guard knows more clashes, and motifs move along the key's scale.

### Added

- **The project inside the FLAC** (GitHub issue #9): Export Release's new **project in FLAC** (on by default) writes the release record - the workflow, the approved Song Sheets, score, lyrics, style, seeds and settings - into the song's FLAC, gzip-compressed in a metadata block of its own that players skip (a FLAC *APPLICATION* block, id `PLNO`, layout `plenio.project/1`): about 25 KB for a typical record of 300 KB (at most 35 KB in 856 songs measured). **File > Continue a Plenio song…** opens a song from its FLAC (*Open a record or FLAC…*), and so does a FLAC dropped onto the empty canvas - the browser reads the record from the file's metadata, nothing is uploaded; a FLAC without a record, one dropped onto a node or with Shift held goes to ComfyUI as before. The `.plenio.json` next to the song stays. A file cannot hold its own hash: the record lists a FLAC that carries it with the size and SHA-256 it has without the record (`"project": "embedded"`), so the copy in the FLAC and the `.plenio.json` are the same record, and two renders with the same audio and tags list the same hash; every FLAC entry also has `audio_md5`, the MD5 of the decoded audio from the file's STREAMINFO. Secrets stay redacted as in the record; turn the option off for files you share without their making-of (ComfyUI's `--disable-metadata` turns it off too).

### Changed

- **A cover's melody follows the new chords** (owner's decision): below *song flow closeness* 80 the melody no longer stays frozen while the writer re-harmonises. From 50 a melody note that clashes with a new chord moves onto it (the nearest chord tone on a strong beat or held for a beat, a step otherwise); below 50 Plenio writes the melody anew over the chords - chord tones on the accents, small steps, phrases that end on a chord tone, near the old line at 49 and freer down to 0 (the arrangement seed picks the phrase shape). A sung melody keeps its rhythm, so its syllables stay on their notes; an instrumental's lead line may also get new rhythms below 45 (from 20 its first bar keeps its rhythm). The lower the closeness, the less the old melody holds the writer's chords back - at 20 they win nearly everywhere. The form always stays; from 80, and in songs, the melody stays as before.
- **The writer sees the whole bar's melody**: the score table of *Compose Arrangement* and the re-ask showed only the notes on beats 1 and 3, while the harmony guard weighs every note of the bar - now every note on a beat or an eighth long is shown, those on a strong beat marked (`E* G A* B`).
- **More clashes recognised**: the harmony guard also rejects a melody note a half step under a chord's major seventh (Bb against Cmaj7), the #4 beside a chord's fifth (F# over C) and, on a strong beat or held, a minor third over a major triad - blues, rock and metal keep their blue third as a short passing note, and over a dominant seventh chord (the blues' I7) also held. The instrument lines follow the same rules.
- **Motifs move along the key's scale**: a *motif* figure moved onto another chord keeps its scale steps (C E G over C becomes A C E over Am - before, it moved by semitones and sang A C# E), takes the chord's own tone where the chord alters the scale (G# over E in A minor), and no motif, line or countermelody note clashes on beats 2 and 4 any more.
- **Rests in the notation**: the score editor and the sheet music draw a rest that runs over the middle of an even bar as two (`c2z6z6z2` instead of a dotted half rest hiding beat 3). The score YuE2 reads keeps its own way of writing rests.
