## 1.0.2 - 2026-10-19

**Creativity, one slider.** Song Brief and Cover Brief get a slider from *0 steady* to *100 experimental*. At **50** everything stays exactly as before - also in every workflow and song made earlier - and away from it the writer writes plainer or bolder lyrics and a more specific style, while YuE2 plans and performs more steadily or more freely. The Plenio Player shows each song's creativity and the sampling it gave, and filters by it. ComfyUI 0.41.0 is now the version Plenio is tested against.

- **Creativity**: 0 steady, 50 the reference, 100 experimental - the writer's rules and temperature, a creative mode's plan, YuE2's plan and takes; moderate ends, the repetition penalties untouched.
- **A more varied style**: for YuE2 the same short line made more specific (YuE2 sings long style prose), for MiniMax Music 3 a caption that tells an arc of contrasts.
- **In the player**: the creativity and the sampling on a song's page, a *Creativity* filter and `creativity:>60` in the search.
- **ComfyUI 0.41.0** is the reference; 0.37.0 stays the minimum.

### Added

- **Creativity** (owner's request: a very simple way to more creative songs): one slider on Song Brief and Cover Brief from **0 steady** to **100 experimental**; **50 is the reference** - the writer's prompt, every temperature and YuE2's sampling exactly as before, the brief's fingerprint too - and a workflow saved before the slider (or an older API prompt) keeps 50. Away from 40-60 the writer gets rules for the lyrics and the style by level (*steady*, *restrained*, *varied*, *experimental*): plain and typical at the low end; at the high end fresher images, varied lines, and a **more varied style** - for YuE2 the same short descriptor line made more specific (named instruments and playing styles, a production colour, words for contrast and movement; YuE2 sang longer style prose), for MiniMax Music 3 a caption whose *Arrangement* part tells an arc of contrasts; *experimental* adds one unexpected but fitting colour and may add one contrasting section. The temperature of the writer's first draft (0.6-0.8-1.0) and of a creative mode's plan (0.55-0.7-0.9) follow it, and in the templates YuE2's plan (temperature 0.55-0.7-0.92, top-p 0.85-0.9-0.95, top-k 20-30-50) and takes (0.9-1.0-1.1, 0.92-0.95-0.97, 60-100-150) - moderate ends, unchanged repetition penalties, MiniMax's own sampling unchanged. A new node **Plenio · Creativity** turns the value into one stage's sampling inside the template blocks; YuE2 Plan, Render and Takes have a *creativity* input linked from the brief; the slider is in App mode; `{creativity}` for tags. **In the player** (owner's request): a song's *Details* show the creativity and the sampling it gave (the writer's and a creative mode's temperature, YuE2's plan and take - marked where they are the reference), and the filters have a *Creativity* row (steady, restrained, balanced, varied, experimental) and the search `creativity:>60`, `creativity:varied`; songs made before the slider count as 50 (balanced). Guide: [Creativity](docs/user/concepts/creativity.md); research and decisions: [creativity.md](docs/design/creativity.md).

### Changed

- **ComfyUI 0.41.0 is the reference** (owner's decision): the CI's contract and host tests run against it, the node snapshot is taken from it, and the templates carry its Generate Text with the new `system_prompt` input and `thinking` output (unused). 0.37.0 stays the minimum: the contract and host tests pass against it too.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-18

**The player at home and at the party.** Two days after 1.0, the Plenio Player opens to every device in your home network - ComfyUI with it -, puts the live playlist on the guests' phones and the visual on the big screen, makes videos and shared players in the background while you listen, shares a library of any size (in parts for a phone), finds a song by every value it has and by tags of your own, and changes from one visual to the next softly. A song's video gets the cover all the way through, the lyrics word by word and lines that come in on the beat, and five new Plenio Drop presets draw the song itself. The song templates get a Plan seed and a Cover seed.

- **The player in your network**: a link with a code of its own (and a QR code) opens the whole player on the phone, the tablet or another computer at home - and ComfyUI too, when it runs; a browser without the link does not get in.
- **Guests at the party**: the live playlist on the guests' phones - to look at, or to change and wish for songs; **Show the visual** puts it on the big screen with their QR code over it.
- **In the making**: videos and shared players made in the background, one after the other, in a list that shows how far each is - with *Stop*.
- **Large shared players**: a file of 2.6 GB (365 songs) shows its songs in under a second and holds about 150 MB once open; parts of 250 MB, 500 MB or 1 GB for phones.
- **Find any song**: every value in the filters, numbers that follow the filters, songs left out with a right click, suggestions as you type, saved filters - and **tags of your own**.
- **Soft changes between visuals**: *Morph*, *Melt*, a soft fade, a zoom, a slide or a flash on the beat, over 0.5 to 8 seconds.
- **More for a song's video**: the cover all the way through (also as a spinning record), the lyrics word by word, in places of their own and coming in on the beat; *Cover Galaxy*, *Title Smoke*, *Lyric Tunnel*, *Album Kaleido* and *Cover Echo* draw the cover, the title and the words into the picture.

### Added

- **Guests in your network** (owner's request): in Live Play, *Let guests see the live playlist* gives everyone on the same network (the party's Wi-Fi) a page of their own on the phone - the song that plays, what comes next, what played. The host chooses whether guests *only look* or *change it*: move songs up and down, take them out, and add songs from the library next or at the end. The way in is a QR code and an address with a random part; **New address** makes another at any time and the old one leads nowhere. The screen shows the QR code, the address, both or nothing - as a card in Live Play and in Now playing, and **Show it big** over everything. The access is a small server of its own on port 8770 that serves only the guests' page, the playlist with titles and covers and, in change mode, the library's titles - no audio, no files, no paths; the player itself still answers this computer only (`plenio/player/guest.py`, `guest_page.html`; the page carries out the wishes, `frontend/src/player/guests.ts`; QR codes by an encoder of Plenio's own, `qr.ts`, checked against OpenCV's decoder for every version it makes). `--guests-host` sets where it listens.
- **Every field of the brief on a song's page** (owner's request): *Details > The song* lists the work mode, template, genre, mood, tempo, key, meter, length and target length, vocals, language, voice, lyrics theme (or melody and lead instrument), a cover's harmony and closeness sliders, the arrangement, genre closeness and lines under the singing - a field left empty with the brief's default and *(default)*, or *open - the writer chose*; a template's value with *(from the template)*.
- **The player's version at the top of Settings** (owner's request), and a note when the page open is from another version.
- **In the making: videos and shared players in the background** (owner's request): *Make the video* and *Make the player* put them in a queue - each kind one after the other, a video and a shared player side by side - and **In the making** in the sidebar lists them with how far each is, **Stop**, and the files when done. Videos are drawn by the page and go on with it in the background (at full speed: the drawing pauses no longer wait for the browser's timers, which it holds back in a background tab; the page asks before it closes while one is made); shared players are made by the player and go on when the page closes (`GET /api/package-jobs`, `POST /api/package/<job>/cancel`).
- **Large shared players open, also on a phone** (owner's request: a 2 GB file on a smartphone): the file is laid out to be read from the top (`plenio.portable/2`) - the songs' list and the player first, which start at once, then each song's page, the covers and the sound in pieces the page turns into files of the browser's own and lets go. Measured on 365 songs (2.6 GB): the list shows after 0.7 s, every song has arrived after 9-15 s and plays as soon as its sound is there, and the page holds about 150 MB afterwards (the first format: 9 GB while opening, 6.9 GB after). A browser holds an HTML file in memory while it reads it - 2.8 GB for a few seconds for that file - so a large share can be made in **parts** of at most 250 MB, 500 MB or 1 GB, each a whole player of its songs, sharing the listener's favourites and playlists (a 500 MB part opens with under 600 MB). The file is written as it is made (it was put together in memory: 2.6 GB took as much), and the MP3s are made in up to eight threads (365 songs in about five minutes instead of twenty).
- **Soft changes between visuals, to choose** (owner's request: not a sudden start - and one that brings both together, organic and soft whatever the kind): both visuals play on while they change, over 0.5-8 seconds (3 by default) - *Morph* (both together, each as bright as the other half way, melting into one another), *Melt* (soft drifting blots, like ink in water), *Soft fade*, *Zoom through*, *Slide* (behind a soft edge), *Flash on the beat*, *A different one each time*, or *Cut*; every soft change swirls the frame before over the new one, so they flow into each other like smoke. Between Plenio Drop visuals the glide takes as long, and a Drop visual that starts after another kind starts from black (it showed an old picture). In the visuals' settings and in a song's video (`visuals/mixer.ts`).
- **More for a song's video** (owner's request: the cover always, more lively text animations in time with the music and in places of their own, more MilkDrop-like visuals with the text and the cover in them): **the cover all the way through** - in a corner, at a side, in the middle or drifting slowly, its size, rounded/square/round or **a spinning record**, pulsing with the beat; the lyrics **word by word** (the word sung now large, popping in on a little tilt, the ones before it smaller above), subtitles and karaoke **where** you want them (bottom, middle, top, or **a new place every line**, never the one before, slightly tilted), each line **coming in** with a fade, a rise, a zoom, a drop with a bounce, a blur, a wipe or a glitch (or a different one every line), **on the beat** (in one beat of the song's tempo, the lyrics pulsing with it); **into the visual**: the title at the start, the line sung and the cover drawn into a Plenio Drop visual's smoke, melting and swirling with it (a glow over a classic one).
- **The song in Plenio Drop** (owner's request): presets draw **pictures** (`sprites`) into the feedback - the song's `cover`, its `title`, the `line` and the `word` sung - with the new formula names `songtime`, `line_on`, `line_age`; five new presets use them, in the player and in videos: *Cover Galaxy*, *Title Smoke*, *Lyric Tunnel*, *Album Kaleido*, *Cover Echo* (21 presets). The guide has a section on pictures.
- **The party on the big screen** (owner's request): **Show the visual** in Live Play opens the visual on the whole screen; the guests' QR code over it is a choice of its own (on by default), with its corner and size (large for a room to scan) - in Live Play's guests' settings and in the visuals' settings.
- **The player in your network, and ComfyUI** (owner's request: a simple link to the whole player from the home network, shown in the player; ComfyUI in the browser when it runs): **Settings > In your network** opens the whole player to every device in the home network by a link with a code of its own (a QR code) - the browser that opened it once comes in by the address alone, nobody else does, and **New link** shuts every other one out. ComfyUI too, by a second link, when it runs on the computer (it need not listen on the network itself; its WebSocket goes through; *Open in ComfyUI* works from there). From the network the player does all but choose its folders, stop, open folders on the computer's screen and delete songs for good. Two small gateways of the player's own on ports 8780-8789; the player still answers the computer only (`plenio/player/network.py`, `--network-host`). *ComfyUI ↗* in the sidebar opens ComfyUI when it runs.
- **Two devices at once keep both their changes**: the player counts the state's changes; a page that saved after another (a phone and the computer, or two tabs) merges the two - a playlist from the side that changed it, favourites and tags with both sides' additions and removals, plays added up, each setting from the side that changed it - and every page takes in another's changes within a few seconds (it overwrote them before).
- **Tags of your own** (owner's request): every song can have tags - on its page, in its menu, or for every song a filter shows at once (**Tag…**); the tags given before are offered as you type. Find them with `#party` or `tag:party` and in a filter row of their own; the cards show them (a click shows the songs with one); **Settings > Tags** renames (into another tag: they become one) and deletes them, and the filters that name a tag follow. Kept with the playlists (`state.json`, backups), and *Share as a player* can take them along.
- **Every value in the filters** (owner's report: *qawwali (from the template)* was in a song's details but not among the filter chips - the genre chips showed the 10 most common, in 365 songs of 125 genres): every row opens to all its values (**+ 113 more**, with a field to find one). New rows: tempo and length ranges, key, template, what a song has (timed lyrics, sheet music, notation, an unmastered take, a cover picture) and listening (favourites, played, never played, no tags yet); the rows past the first five behind **More filters**. A right click on a chip **leaves those songs out** (*not Genre: jazz*); the numbers on the chips follow the filters (owner's report: they were always of every song) - how many of the songs shown have each value, so they say how many would stay, and values none of them has are gone; a row of one choice (Plenio version, tempo, length) keeps its other values to switch to. **Suggestions as you type**: `gen` -> `genre:`, `genre:blu` -> every genre with *blu* and its count, `#` -> your tags. **Saved filters** in the sidebar (*Save this filter…*). New search words: `is:played`, `is:unplayed`, `is:tagged`, `is:untagged`, `has:score`; `has:timing` also counts timed lyrics from subtitle files.
- **Back to the songs, the song that plays is in sight** (owner's request: with 1000 songs, not from the top again): coming back from a song's page or another view, the songs' page goes to the card of the song that plays when it is not in sight where the list was left - the list grows by *Show more*'s steps until it holds it - and the card lights up for a moment.

### Changed

- **Seeds in the templates** (owner's request: the seed node wherever it helps): *1 · YuE2 · Song* has a **Plan seed** (Plenio · Seed, 🔒 by default, also in App mode) under YuE2's plan - 🎲 plans another melody and form for the same lyrics; every song template's Cover Art block has a **Cover seed** (🔒 0, as its field had it) - 🎲 for another picture. Refine keeps its plain field (another seed changes nothing you would hear). *Continue a Plenio song* puts an older record's plan seed and cover seed - from inside the blocks - on the new seed nodes, so a song continued keeps its plan and its cover (all 13 such records of the owner's 142 do).
- **Tidier templates**: checked in the real frontend for nodes on top of each other or across a group's edge - *2 · YuE2 · Cover*'s *Adapter for instrumental covers* stood across the MUSIC MODEL group's edge (now under the adapter) and its *5 · TEXT* group sat a little lower than the others.
- **The small visual in the bar** is a square and bright at any window size (owner's report: a dark strip, stretched on a wide window): a ring of the music round a core that pulses with the bass, breathing slowly when the music stops.
- **The player in a narrow or short window** (a design review, owner's request): below 860 px the sidebar was gone with no way to Playlists, Live Play or Settings - it is now a drawer behind a menu button; in a short window the sidebar scrolls instead of cutting off its foot; the bar gives its right side the room its buttons need and hides the volume slider and the speed below 1100 px (they overlapped the controls at 1024 px); the queue ends above the bar (it covered the bar's buttons, its own among them); no page is wider than the window; the guests' card in Now playing shows only the QR code in a narrower window.

### Fixed

- The songs' page forgot *Show more*: back from a song's page it showed the first 120 songs again, so the place you left could be out of reach; and while it was open, a song that counted a play or arrived from ComfyUI shrank the list back to 120. Only a new search, other filters or another sort start with the first songs now.
- A player started from ComfyUI for a state folder of its own read the default state folder's chosen music folders (`launch.start_player` now hands the folder to the player).
- ComfyUI's **Plenio Player** button took any player running on the player's ports - also one started for another state folder, with another library; it now takes the player of its own state folder and starts one when there is none.
