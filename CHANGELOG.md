## Unreleased

### Added

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-18

**The player at home and at the party.** Two days after 1.0, the Plenio Player opens to every device in your home network - ComfyUI with it -, puts the live playlist on the guests' phones and the visual on the big screen, makes videos and shared players in the background while you listen, shares a library of any size (in parts for a phone), finds a song by every value it has and by tags of your own, and changes from one visual to the next softly. A song's video gets the cover all the way through, the lyrics word by word and lines that come in on the beat, and five new Plenio Drop presets draw the song itself. The song templates get a Plan seed and a Cover seed.

- **The player in your network**: a link with a code of its own (and a QR code) opens the whole player on the phone, the tablet or another computer at home - and ComfyUI too, when it runs; a browser without the link does not get in.
- **Guests at the party**: the live playlist on the guests' phones - to look at, or to change and wish for songs; **Show the visual** puts it on the big screen with their QR code over it.
- **In the making**: videos and shared players made in the background, one after the other, in a list that shows how far each is - with *Stop*.
- **Large shared players**: a file of 2.6 GB (365 songs) shows its songs in under a second and holds about 150 MB once open; parts of 250 MB, 500 MB or 1 GB for phones.
- **Find any song**: every value in the filters, numbers that follow the filters, songs left out with a right click, suggestions as you type, saved filters - and **tags of your own**.
- **Soft changes between visuals**: *Morph*, *Melt*, a soft fade, a zoom, a slide or a flash on the beat, over 0.5 to 8 seconds.
- **More for a song's video**: the cover all the way through (also as a spinning record), the lyrics word by word, in places of their own and coming in on the beat; *Cover Galaxy*, *Title Smoke*, *Lyric Tunnel*, *Album Kaleido* and *Cover Echo* draw the cover, the title and the words into the picture.

### Added

- **Guests in your network** (owner's request): in Live Play, *Let guests see the live playlist* gives everyone on the same network (the party's Wi-Fi) a page of their own on the phone - the song that plays, what comes next, what played. The host chooses whether guests *only look* or *change it*: move songs up and down, take them out, and add songs from the library next or at the end. The way in is a QR code and an address with a random part; **New address** makes another at any time and the old one leads nowhere. The screen shows the QR code, the address, both or nothing - as a card in Live Play and in Now playing, and **Show it big** over everything. The access is a small server of its own on port 8770 that serves only the guests' page, the playlist with titles and covers and, in change mode, the library's titles - no audio, no files, no paths; the player itself still answers this computer only (`plenio/player/guest.py`, `guest_page.html`; the page carries out the wishes, `frontend/src/player/guests.ts`; QR codes by an encoder of Plenio's own, `qr.ts`, checked against OpenCV's decoder for every version it makes). `--guests-host` sets where it listens.
- **Every field of the brief on a song's page** (owner's request): *Details > The song* lists the work mode, template, genre, mood, tempo, key, meter, length and target length, vocals, language, voice, lyrics theme (or melody and lead instrument), a cover's harmony and closeness sliders, the arrangement, genre closeness and lines under the singing - a field left empty with the brief's default and *(default)*, or *open - the writer chose*; a template's value with *(from the template)*.
- **The player's version at the top of Settings** (owner's request), and a note when the page open is from another version.
- **In the making: videos and shared players in the background** (owner's request): *Make the video* and *Make the player* put them in a queue - each kind one after the other, a video and a shared player side by side - and **In the making** in the sidebar lists them with how far each is, **Stop**, and the files when done. Videos are drawn by the page and go on with it in the background (at full speed: the drawing pauses no longer wait for the browser's timers, which it holds back in a background tab; the page asks before it closes while one is made); shared players are made by the player and go on when the page closes (`GET /api/package-jobs`, `POST /api/package/<job>/cancel`).
- **Large shared players open, also on a phone** (owner's request: a 2 GB file on a smartphone): the file is laid out to be read from the top (`plenio.portable/2`) - the songs' list and the player first, which start at once, then each song's page, the covers and the sound in pieces the page turns into files of the browser's own and lets go. Measured on 365 songs (2.6 GB): the list shows after 0.7 s, every song has arrived after 9-15 s and plays as soon as its sound is there, and the page holds about 150 MB afterwards (the first format: 9 GB while opening, 6.9 GB after). A browser holds an HTML file in memory while it reads it - 2.8 GB for a few seconds for that file - so a large share can be made in **parts** of at most 250 MB, 500 MB or 1 GB, each a whole player of its songs, sharing the listener's favourites and playlists (a 500 MB part opens with under 600 MB). The file is written as it is made (it was put together in memory: 2.6 GB took as much), and the MP3s are made in up to eight threads (365 songs in about five minutes instead of twenty).
- **Soft changes between visuals, to choose** (owner's request: not a sudden start - and one that brings both together, organic and soft whatever the kind): both visuals play on while they change, over 0.5-8 seconds (3 by default) - *Morph* (both together, each as bright as the other half way, melting into one another), *Melt* (soft drifting blots, like ink in water), *Soft fade*, *Zoom through*, *Slide* (behind a soft edge), *Flash on the beat*, *A different one each time*, or *Cut*; every soft change swirls the frame before over the new one, so they flow into each other like smoke. Between Plenio Drop visuals the glide takes as long, and a Drop visual that starts after another kind starts from black (it showed an old picture). In the visuals' settings and in a song's video (`visuals/mixer.ts`).
- **More for a song's video** (owner's request: the cover always, more lively text animations in time with the music and in places of their own, more MilkDrop-like visuals with the text and the cover in them): **the cover all the way through** - in a corner, at a side, in the middle or drifting slowly, its size, rounded/square/round or **a spinning record**, pulsing with the beat; the lyrics **word by word** (the word sung now large, popping in on a little tilt, the ones before it smaller above), subtitles and karaoke **where** you want them (bottom, middle, top, or **a new place every line**, never the one before, slightly tilted), each line **coming in** with a fade, a rise, a zoom, a drop with a bounce, a blur, a wipe or a glitch (or a different one every line), **on the beat** (in one beat of the song's tempo, the lyrics pulsing with it); **into the visual**: the title at the start, the line sung and the cover drawn into a Plenio Drop visual's smoke, melting and swirling with it (a glow over a classic one).
- **The song in Plenio Drop** (owner's request): presets draw **pictures** (`sprites`) into the feedback - the song's `cover`, its `title`, the `line` and the `word` sung - with the new formula names `songtime`, `line_on`, `line_age`; five new presets use them, in the player and in videos: *Cover Galaxy*, *Title Smoke*, *Lyric Tunnel*, *Album Kaleido*, *Cover Echo* (21 presets). The guide has a section on pictures.
- **The party on the big screen** (owner's request): **Show the visual** in Live Play opens the visual on the whole screen; the guests' QR code over it is a choice of its own (on by default), with its corner and size (large for a room to scan) - in Live Play's guests' settings and in the visuals' settings.
- **The player in your network, and ComfyUI** (owner's request: a simple link to the whole player from the home network, shown in the player; ComfyUI in the browser when it runs): **Settings > In your network** opens the whole player to every device in the home network by a link with a code of its own (a QR code) - the browser that opened it once comes in by the address alone, nobody else does, and **New link** shuts every other one out. ComfyUI too, by a second link, when it runs on the computer (it need not listen on the network itself; its WebSocket goes through; *Open in ComfyUI* works from there). Two small gateways of the player's own on ports 8780-8789; the player still answers the computer only (`plenio/player/network.py`, `--network-host`). *ComfyUI ↗* in the sidebar opens ComfyUI when it runs.

- **Tags of your own** (owner's request): every song can have tags - on its page, in its menu, or for every song a filter shows at once (**Tag…**); the tags given before are offered as you type. Find them with `#party` or `tag:party` and in a filter row of their own; the cards show them (a click shows the songs with one); **Settings > Tags** renames (into another tag: they become one) and deletes them, and the filters that name a tag follow. Kept with the playlists (`state.json`, backups), and *Share as a player* can take them along.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-16

**Plenio 1.0.** The whole way from an idea to a song people listen to, on your own computer and free: a local language model writes the song (or you compose it yourself), YuE2 or MiniMax Music 3 performs it, Plenio masters it and exports it with its tags, its lyrics as subtitles and its whole project - and now the **Plenio Player** plays it, shows it and shares it. After fourteen published versions in three weeks, the templates, the nodes and the release record have settled: 1.0.0 is the version to build on, and saved workflows and release records keep working in every 1.x version. (0.8.1 was not published; everything it had is in 1.0.0.)

- **The Plenio Player**: your songs in the browser, without ComfyUI - one click from ComfyUI, search, a page per song, the lyrics lit up as they are sung and the sheet music following the music, playlists, Winamp-style visuals.
- **Plenio Drop**: MilkDrop-style visuals of Plenio's own on the graphics card - 16 presets, one made up anew each time, and presets of your own as a short JSON file.
- **Live Play**: a playlist for a party that you change while it plays; the songs you make in ComfyUI join it as soon as they are exported.
- **A song as a video**: an MP4 of the visual with the lyrics (subtitles or karaoke), the notation following the music and a title card - Full HD, 4K, square or upright for Shorts.
- **Share your songs as a player**: one HTML file with the player and the songs as MP3 - anyone double-clicks it and listens, with lyrics, notation and visuals, no install.
- **Lyrics subtitles timed by listening**, tags filled from the brief with variables, seeds with one plain switch, wider briefs and short tooltips beside the node.

### Added

- **Plenio Player** (owner's request): a local website for the songs you made - `python plenio_player.py` (or `python -m plenio.player`) with ComfyUI's Python opens it at `http://127.0.0.1:8765/`, ComfyUI need not run. It reads every release record below ComfyUI's output folder (`--folder` for others); playlists, favourites, play counts and settings live in `~/.plenio/player`.
  - **Library**: a featured song, search over titles, lyrics, genres, moods and briefs with field filters (`genre:jazz`, `bpm:>110`, `key:Am`, `model:yue2`, `lang:german`, `is:fav`, `has:pdf`, `"phrases"`, `-word` ...), filter chips for the common genres, moods, models, languages and creative modes, eight sorts, grid or list; songs without a cover get artwork from their colours.
  - **A song's page**: it follows the music (the next song's page on the same tab, Back unchanged); the lyrics (click a line to hear it, the line sung now stays in view), the notation following the music (the line of music sounding now stays in view, also after a jump; the cursor at the notes sounding - at the bar's chord only in a bar with no note, one note or only a rest; click a note to jump), the sheet music PDF, every detail (the brief, model and writer, loudness, the seeds with a copy button, the tags, the cover's prompt), all files to download, the song's other takes and songs alike.
  - **Now playing**: Stage, Lyrics (karaoke, syllable by syllable), Notation (the notes sounding light up, the sheet scrolls along) and Visual (the visual full screen, the lyrics as subtitles, the controls fading away); the sections as chips to jump to; the waveform as the seek bar with the sections and, under the mouse, the line sung there; a title card per song. Crossfades between songs, **A/B** with the unmastered take (in step, no gap), speed 0.75-1.25× keeping the pitch, resume where you stopped, the system's media keys.
  - **Visuals** after Winamp, MilkDrop and AVS: 13 visuals (Classic Bars, Oscilloscope, Stereo Vectorscope, Radial Bloom, Feedback Tunnel, Hyperspace, Plasma, Inferno, Kaleidoscope, Beat Fountain, Spectrum Ridges, Ripples, Retro Grid) drawn from the spectrum, the waveform, the stereo image and the beats. Modes: *match the music* (by genre, mood and tempo), *random* from the visuals you tick, *cycle*, *fixed*; a visual of its own per song and per playlist (the song's wins, then the playlist's); a change with every song, at every section, every few seconds or when the music drops in; five colour looks and a sensitivity.
  - **Plenio Drop** (owner's request: visuals like Winamp's MilkDrop, made anew): a renderer of Plenio's own on the graphics card (WebGL2) with MilkDrop's feedback loop - every frame draws the last one again, zoomed, turned, bent by one of six patterns (swirl, waves, tunnel, kaleidoscope, ripple), fading - and the music on top (waves, shapes, motion vectors), then echo, mirrors and colour effects. 16 presets (Nebula Swirl, Star Gate, Kaleido Bloom, Shockwave, Ink in Water ...) and *Drop · Random*, a new one each time; a change of preset glides, the picture stays. They are visuals like the others (same modes, per song, per playlist, *all*/*none* per group); a quality setting (*Fast*, *Balanced*, *Sharp*); 60 frames a second on an ordinary graphics card, the same speed at any frame rate.
  - **Presets of your own** (owner's request: a format simple to understand and well documented): a preset is a short JSON file of numbers and small formulas (`"zoom": "1.01 + 0.02 * bass"`), read by the player's own calculator - never run as script, so a shared preset can only draw. Put it in `~/.plenio/player/visuals`; *Edit a copy* saves the visual that plays there to change; a saved file changes on screen within three seconds; a mistake is shown with where and why. Guide with every parameter, the music's names, the functions and recipes: [Plenio Drop: visuals of your own](docs/user/concepts/player-visuals.md).
  - **The visual on the whole screen**: Shift+F, a double-click on the visual, *full screen* in the visuals' settings, or the button on the bar's small visual.
  - **Several folders, and other music** (owner's request): Settings > Songs lists the folders the player reads; **Add a folder…** chooses one with a folder browser, × stops reading one, and they are kept with the player (`library.json`), also when ComfyUI starts it. Per folder, **also other music** plays the audio files not made with Plenio (FLAC, MP3, WAV, OGG, Opus, M4A) - clearly marked (*other music* on the card and the page, the filter *Made with*, `is:other`), with what their files have: title, artist, album, genre and year from the tags, the embedded or the folder's cover, the lyrics from the tags, and timed lyrics word by word from an `.lrc` beside the file. No notation, brief or *Open in ComfyUI* - that needs a release record. A Plenio song's files (audio, unmastered take, stems) always stay its own.
  - **One click from ComfyUI** (owner's request): **Plenio Player** in ComfyUI's action bar beside *Run*, in a new **Plenio** menu and the command palette, and **▶ Open in the Plenio Player** on Export Release (at the song just exported). ComfyUI does not serve the player: it finds the one running on this computer or starts it - as a program of its own, which keeps running when ComfyUI stops (`POST /plenio/player/open`). Without ComfyUI: double-click `Plenio Player.bat` (`plenio_player.sh`).
  - **Open in ComfyUI** (owner's request): a song's page opens the song's workflow in ComfyUI to work on it - its brief, lyrics, score and settings, as *Continue a Plenio song* restores them; the record goes from the player to the ComfyUI tab.
  - **Settings that last, and say so** (owner's request): everything the page remembers is kept by the player on this computer (`~/.plenio/player/state.json`), also the last view of Now playing, a song page's last tab and the library's search and filter chips (per view, with a *Filtered* row and **Reset filters**; the library keeps its place in the list when you come back), saved half a second after a change and when the page closes. A **Settings** page says what is kept and where, when it was saved, and offers a backup, a restore and a fresh start; it also shows where *Open in ComfyUI* goes and has *Stop the player*.
  - **The song's page easier to find**: a click on a card opens it (▶ plays), ⓘ in the bar and in Now playing, the key **I**.
  - **Remove a song** (owner's request): 👎 *Remove…* on its page or in its menu lists the song's files and moves them to the retired songs (`_retired` in the library folder, which the player does not read; move them back to have the song again) or deletes them for good after asking once more. The song leaves the queue, the playlists and the favourites; only the files in the song's own folder are touched.
  - **Live Play** (owner's request: make songs at a party and they play): a live playlist that plays while you change it - drag to reorder, play now, play next, take out, add from the library, save as a playlist - and the songs ComfyUI makes join it as soon as Export Release has written them: **next**, **at the end** or **anywhere at random** (or only announced). A message and a red *new* mark show each arrival. At its end it starts again from the top or **waits for the next new song** and plays it at once. Start it with the queue, a playlist, the newest songs or empty; it stays on when the page is opened again (`GET /api/new?since=`: the songs that appeared since the last answer, the folders read again at most every two seconds).
  - **Save a song as a video** (owner's request): **Video…** on a song's page makes an MP4 (H.264, AAC) - Full HD, HD, 4K, square or upright (Shorts, Reels, TikTok), 30 or 60 fps, the whole song or a part (faded in and out), any visual or the one the settings choose, a new one at every section if wanted, the colours; over it the lyrics as subtitles or karaoke (lit up as sung), the notation with its cursor and the notes aglow, a title card with the cover, the song in a corner, a progress bar with the sections, *made with Plenio*; the master or the unmastered take. A preview frame; the frames drawn from the song's own sound (the visuals react as when it plays) and encoded by the browser on the graphics card, several times faster than the song lasts; saved in the player's `videos` folder, listed in Settings.
  - *Open the folder* (after a video, a shared player, in Settings) did nothing in a player started from ComfyUI (owner's report): the shell's open did not reach the desktop from a process without a window. Explorer is now started itself, with the new file chosen; a failure is shown. It also no longer leaves the visuals' README in those folders.
  - **Share your songs as a player** (owner's request: give your own music to others very easily): **Share…** in a view or on a playlist, or **Share as a player…**, packs the player and the songs chosen into one HTML file that plays by itself - double-click it, in any current browser, no install, no server, no ComfyUI. The songs go as MP3 of 128, 160 or 192 kbit/s (never FLAC), with their covers (512 px), timed lyrics, notation, waveforms and, if wanted, sheet music, your look, your own visuals and your playlists of them. Everything that needs no Plenio plays there - library, song pages, karaoke, notation, Now playing, visuals, playlists, Live Play, repeat -; the listener's playlists and settings are kept in their browser. No records, workflows or paths go in (`plenio/player/package.py`, `frontend/src/player/portable.ts`).
  - **The notation follows songs without measured times** (owner's report: it often ran a bar behind): for songs exported before 1.0.0 (or with subtitle files only) the cursor followed the score's clock, but a YuE2 render is shifted against its score - about a bar in these songs - and the shift wanders. The player now finds the score in the recording from their harmonies (pitch classes, every 12 seconds; `plenio/player/align.py`, cached) and follows it: on the demo songs the cursor lands within 0.3 s of a line's first syllable instead of 2.8 s. Buttons above the sheet move it by a quarter second or a bar, kept per song.
