## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-22

**Audio HQ and a new ear for the lyrics.** Every take now goes through **Audio HQ** before the EQ: *Stereo Cleanup* calms the unsteady stereo shimmer of AI renders and *Regenerate Highs* lets UniverSR generate fresh highs above 8.2 kHz - presets that follow the song's genre, in every template's Master block and in Enhance & Master. The lyrics are heard by **Qwen3-ASR** and timed word by word by **Qwen3-ForcedAligner**, for covers and for the lyrics subtitles; faster-whisper stays as the fallback for other languages. The System Check says where every model file and asset goes.

- **Audio HQ**: HQ 1 · Stereo Cleanup and HQ 2 · Regenerate Highs on, HQ 3 · De-Reverb bypassed; presets *auto* (from the brief's genre), *standard*, *gentle*, *strong*, *wide*, *custom*; about 1.5 s per second of music for HQ 2 on an RTX 5060 Ti.
- **Qwen3-ASR** with its forced aligner is the lyrics ASR (Transcribe Lyrics, subtitles); Plenio carries its inference code and installs nothing.
- **All audio work after the render lives in the Master block**; the REFINE block left the templates, and every template needs the UniverSR file (229 MB).
- **System Check**: folders, download links and the Plenio assets with their folders.


### Added

- **Audio HQ: three refinement stages before the EQ, in every template** (owner's request, after a listening test of the *Plenio-AudioHQ v3.0* proposal on YuE2 takes). Inside the **Plenio · Master** block the take now runs through **HQ 1 · Stereo Cleanup** (a high-cut and a spectral cleanup of the *side* signal above 2 kHz - the unsteady stereo shimmer of AI renders - and a little 2x oversampled harmonic warmth; the middle is not filtered), **HQ 2 · Regenerate Highs** (*erase and regenerate*: the UniverSR model generates new highs from the song without its top octaves, and above 8.2 kHz they replace most of the original's at the original's level; below the crossover nothing changes) and **HQ 3 · De-Reverb** (the short metallic room and a phase lock - bypassed: it also lowers the highs by 3-4 dB), then the EQ and Loudness & Dynamics as before. **4 · Enhance & Master** has the three stages as plain nodes, with their presets in App mode. Every stage has the same presets - **auto** (the default: the brief's genre picks the profile), *standard - pop, hip-hop, soul, world*, *gentle - classical, jazz, folk*, *strong - rock, metal, punk*, *wide - electronic, dance, ambient* - and *custom* for its fields; the summary says which profile ran and why (`strong (auto: genre 'thrash metal' (metal))`). **HQ 2 regenerates from 8.2 kHz in every preset** - the setting the owner listened to (a first build let *gentle* start at 12.2 kHz, and the templates' default singer-songwriter song changed too little to hear). *standard* is the proposal the owner listened to - HQ 2 with UniverSR, which sounded better than the proposal's FlashSR (Plenio offers no FlashSR); the other profiles are marked provisional. The code follows the proposal's DSP (checked to -140 dB) without its whole-song soft clipper and without its silent fallback for a missing model (it cut the highs by 15 dB). **Time:** HQ 2 takes about 1.5 s per second of music on an RTX 5060 Ti - UniverSR in bfloat16, and the 40 ms level envelope as an FFT convolution (it took 19 of 21 seconds per 30 s of music); its advanced *model steps* trade highs for time (3: about 1.1 s, 2: about 0.75 s). Guide: [Audio HQ](docs/user/concepts/audio-hq.md); measurements: [test report](docs/test-reports/2026-10-21-audio-hq.md).
- **Qwen3-ASR is the lyrics ASR** (owner's request): **Qwen3-ASR 1.7B** hears the sung words and **Qwen3-ForcedAligner 0.6B** gives every word its time - in *Transcribe Lyrics* (covers with the original lyrics, *Check sung lyrics*) and in Export Release's lyrics subtitles, so the subtitles take the aligner's exact times instead of Whisper's corrected ones. The two models run one after the other in Plenio's ASR worker (about 4 GB of VRAM at a time; a four-minute song in about half a minute on an RTX 5060 Ti) and come as Plenio assets (Apache-2.0, 4.1 + 1.8 GB, fetched on first use). transformers supports the model only from 5.13 on and ComfyUI ships an older 5.x, so Plenio carries its inference code (`plenio/third_party/qwen3_asr`, vendored from transformers 5.17 by `tools/vendor_qwen3_asr.py`; the same words and times as transformers 5.17 on a test song) and installs nothing. **faster-whisper large-v3 stays as the fallback**: for a language the aligner does not know (it times English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Cantonese, Japanese and Korean) Whisper takes over by itself and the summary says so; *Transcribe Lyrics* still offers it, so workflows saved with it keep running. Word confidence (the editor's unsure words) comes from the transcription's token probabilities.
- **System Check says where every file goes** (owner's request): the model files are listed with their folder (`models/<folder>` inside the models folder it names), the templates that use them and a download link; the **Plenio assets** - Qwen3-ASR, its aligner, the Whisper fallback - with the folder Plenio keeps them in, size, licence, what they are for and whether they are installed.

### Changed

- **All audio work after the render lives in the Master block** (owner's request): the REFINE block (UniverSR, on in *3 · MiniMax · Song*, bypassed elsewhere) is gone from the templates; HQ 2 regenerates the highs with the same model. **The UniverSR file (229 MB) is now needed by every template** - ComfyUI offers the download when a template opens; bypass HQ 2 inside the Master block to run without it. *Refine (48 kHz)* stays in the node library. The Master block has a *brief* input (for *auto*) and three more reports (`hq1_report`, `hq2_report`, `hq3_report`), which reach the release record.
- **UniverSR runs in bfloat16** on a GPU that has it (HQ 2 and Refine): a quarter faster, the result within -25 dB of float32.

### Fixed

- **The brief's creativity reaches every block, also in a workflow saved before 1.0.2** (owner's report: creativity 100 on the Song Brief of a workflow opened through the player on another device, and the song's plan and take sampling were still 50's). ComfyUI keeps a block's inner graph in the workflow, so a workflow saved earlier - a tab the browser restores, a file, a release record - kept Write Song, Arrange and the YuE2 blocks with fixed sampling numbers, while its brief already had the slider: it changed the writer's rules only. Such a workflow now gets the current blocks as it opens (`core.blocks`, route `/plenio/workflow/blocks`, `frontend/src/extension/blocks.ts` before the frontend configures it), and the YuE2 blocks' *creativity* is linked from the brief - in the graph, as in the templates; a toast says which blocks changed, and at 50 they give exactly the numbers they had. A block whose sampling was changed inside it, or whose inputs differ from the current one's, is kept and named in the toast. The player marks a song's sampling that was *fixed in the workflow, not from the creativity* when the brief asked for another value.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-10-19

**Creativity, one slider.** Song Brief and Cover Brief get a slider from *0 steady* to *100 experimental*. At **50** everything stays exactly as before - also in every workflow and song made earlier - and away from it the writer writes plainer or bolder lyrics and a more specific style, while YuE2 plans and performs more steadily or more freely. The Plenio Player shows each song's creativity and the sampling it gave, and filters by it. ComfyUI 0.41.0 is now the version Plenio is tested against.

- **Creativity**: 0 steady, 50 the reference, 100 experimental - the writer's rules and temperature, a creative mode's plan, YuE2's plan and takes; moderate ends, the repetition penalties untouched.
- **A more varied style**: for YuE2 the same short line made more specific (YuE2 sings long style prose), for MiniMax Music 3 a caption that tells an arc of contrasts.
- **In the player**: the creativity and the sampling on a song's page, a *Creativity* filter and `creativity:>60` in the search.
- **ComfyUI 0.41.0** is the reference; 0.37.0 stays the minimum.

### Added

- **Creativity** (owner's request: a very simple way to more creative songs): one slider on Song Brief and Cover Brief from **0 steady** to **100 experimental**; **50 is the reference** - the writer's prompt, every temperature and YuE2's sampling exactly as before, the brief's fingerprint too - and a workflow saved before the slider (or an older API prompt) keeps 50. Away from 40-60 the writer gets rules for the lyrics and the style by level (*steady*, *restrained*, *varied*, *experimental*): plain and typical at the low end; at the high end fresher images, varied lines, and a **more varied style** - for YuE2 the same short descriptor line made more specific (named instruments and playing styles, a production colour, words for contrast and movement; YuE2 sang longer style prose), for MiniMax Music 3 a caption whose *Arrangement* part tells an arc of contrasts; *experimental* adds one unexpected but fitting colour and may add one contrasting section. The temperature of the writer's first draft (0.6-0.8-1.0) and of a creative mode's plan (0.55-0.7-0.9) follow it, and in the templates YuE2's plan (temperature 0.55-0.7-0.92, top-p 0.85-0.9-0.95, top-k 20-30-50) and takes (0.9-1.0-1.1, 0.92-0.95-0.97, 60-100-150) - moderate ends, unchanged repetition penalties, MiniMax's own sampling unchanged. A new node **Plenio · Creativity** turns the value into one stage's sampling inside the template blocks; YuE2 Plan, Render and Takes have a *creativity* input linked from the brief; the slider is in App mode; `{creativity}` for tags. **In the player** (owner's request): a song's *Details* show the creativity and the sampling it gave (the writer's and a creative mode's temperature, YuE2's plan and take - marked where they are the reference), and the filters have a *Creativity* row (steady, restrained, balanced, varied, experimental) and the search `creativity:>60`, `creativity:varied`; songs made before the slider count as 50 (balanced). Guide: [Creativity](docs/user/concepts/creativity.md); research and decisions: [creativity.md](docs/design/creativity.md).

### Changed

- **ComfyUI 0.41.0 is the reference** (owner's decision): the CI's contract and host tests run against it, the node snapshot is taken from it, and the templates carry its Generate Text with the new `system_prompt` input and `thinking` output (unused). 0.37.0 stays the minimum: the contract and host tests pass against it too.
