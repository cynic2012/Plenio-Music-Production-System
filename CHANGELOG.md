## 1.4.0 - 2026-10-29

**Visuals worked out for every pixel, stars for every song, and a say in what the party hears.** Plenio Nova brings 22 visuals the graphics card works out for every pixel - a black hole, a galaxy, northern lights over a lake, the Mandelbulb, liquid chrome -, in a choice that stays compact with a hundred visuals, and the player offers only the visuals a device draws smoothly. Every song gets stars from 0 to 5: yours by ear, and the measured ones as a faded pre-fill - how much of the lyrics is sung as written, how clean the harmony is, how steady the beat, and what went wrong. Users listen, make songs or help as admins; a new song can wait until its maker heard it or an admin approved it, and the guests hear the party's songs again on their phones. Videos come in seven quality steps or at a bitrate of your own. The player starts ComfyUI the way you last started it and names a model file ComfyUI does not find; a song's page says when, how long and on what it was made. The guests' reactions are kept for every song; only the guests' songs are party songs, and the big screen says which party. Videos give their graphics memory back and try again. The lyrics are there in full to copy again - on the song's page in the player and on a page of their own beside every song - and YuE2 takes LoRAs.

### Added

- **The full lyrics to copy**: *Full lyrics*, beside *As written* and *As sung* on a song's page in the player, shows the lyrics as text with their sections (`[Verse]`, `[Chorus]` …) - what goes into a Song Sheet - with buttons that copy the lyrics, the lyrics without the section tags and the style, and the lyrics as the take sings them below. Before, a song with measured times showed its lyrics only as timed lines. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#a-songs-page)
- **The song's page** (`<name>.html`): Export Release writes everything about the song on one page beside it - the cover and the song to play, the lyrics with their sections, the style, the brief and the cover's prompt with copy buttons, every field of the brief, how it was made (the model files, the LoRAs, the seeds), the sound and its mastering, the score as ABC and links to every file. A double click opens it in any browser, without internet. *song page (HTML)* on the node, on by default; the player shows the same page for every song of its library, also one exported before. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#the-songs-page-html)
- **YuE2 LoRAs**: a node under the music model of the YuE2 templates with up to four LoRAs, each with its strength. It reads from each file whether it shapes YuE2's plan (the planner, CLIP) or its sound (the acoustic model, MODEL) and applies it there; files in m-a-p's original layout - which ComfyUI's own LoRA loader skips - are joined into ComfyUI's fused projections - block-diagonally, `down = [A_q; A_k; A_v]` and `up = diag(B_q, B_k, B_v)`, so `up · down` is exactly the stacked deltas of q, k and v (and of gate and up). A file that does not fit - another model's LoRA, a broken file, weights of another shape or values that are not numbers - is left out and named, and the rest applies; with every slot on *none*, as the templates ship, YuE2 runs as loaded. LoRA training stays outside Plenio for now. [Details](docs/user/concepts/loras.md)
- **Song API 1.2**: the release field `song_page`.
- **How a song was made, on its page**: the date and time, how long making it took, the graphics card (with its memory) or the processor alone, the processor and the RAM, and the lyrics fidelity. Export Release writes them into the release record (`made`; the names of the graphics card and the processor, no serial number, user name or path - [what the record holds](SECURITY.md#plenios-http-routes)); songs exported before show when they were saved and, where their record says it, the fidelity. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#a-songs-page)
- **The guests' reactions kept for every song**, apart from the favourites' hearts: counted in the player's state (`reactions.json`), shown in Live Play and on the song's page (*🔥 12 · ❤️ 4 from the guests*). The host chooses **as many per song as they like** or **one per song and guest** - a tap on another emoji changes it. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#guests-in-your-network)
- The example songs as videos made by the Plenio Player, in the README (*Listen first*) and the getting-started guide; tutorials 9 and 10 in the README's table.
- **The demo page shows Plenio at work**: two films with sound - *Plenio in 30 seconds* (the player playing one song, in step with its lyrics, between ComfyUI's brief, Song Sheet, score editor and EQ and a party's big screen) and *Twelve genres in thirty seconds* -, the song videos, new songs and the archive of more than seventy songs and covers. New songs and song videos are added with `tools/demo_showcase.py` from a song's FLAC or record - its cover, genre, length, tempo, key, style, lyrics and how it was made, nothing else of the record - with SoundCloud, YouTube or its MP3 to play. [How](docs/demo-showcase.md)
- **Plenio Nova: 22 visuals the graphics card works out for every pixel** - flights through hyperspace, neon rings, a city whose towers are the spectrum and an outrun valley; a black hole bending the starlight round its disk, a galaxy, a nebula, northern lights over a lake, the sea under the moon; the Mandelbulb, gyroid caves, liquid chrome, a sound sphere, a plasma globe, stained glass, a laser show, a mandala, silk; and three that grow from frame to frame - ink in water, living patterns (a reaction-diffusion), the song's last seconds as ridge lines (*Pulsar*) and as mountains. Most are ray-marched in 3D; all move with the bass, its kicks, the mids, the highs, the beat, the spectrum and the waveform, take the colour scheme as their palette and go through the same light: a bloom, a filmic tone curve (ACES), a touch of colour fringing on the beat, a vignette and grain. They play live, in the video gallery's previews and in videos. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#visuals)
- **More quality settings for a video**: seven steps from *Low* to *Best*, or a bitrate of one's own (0.5 to 200 Mbit/s) - constant where a platform asks for it; 24, 25, 30, 50 or 60 frames a second; a full picture every 1, 2, 4 or 8 seconds; the encoder (as the browser chooses, the graphics card, the processor); the sound as AAC at 128 to 320 kbit/s; and more sizes (2560 × 1440, 2160 × 2160, 1080 × 1350, 720 × 1280, 2160 × 3840). The dialog says what a setting means in Mbit/s and MB a minute. The picture stays H.264, which every player and platform plays. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#save-a-song-as-a-video)
- **Groups of users**: a user *listens* (may not make songs), *makes songs*, or is an *admin* - a helper with the admin's rights from any device (users and their groups, approvals, the party's settings), to step in when needed; their own playlists and likes stay theirs, and what only the player's computer does stays the computer's. Users set up before are makers. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#who-is-listening-the-login)
- **Before everybody hears a new song**: a song made from the player can wait for its maker to hear it first on their own device - a guest on their phone - and release it or not, and/or for an admin to approve it (*Make a song > Waiting for your approval*, counted in the sidebar). A waiting song is made into a folder of its own the library does not read, so Live Play does not take it in until it is released; one not released is kept aside, nothing is deleted. The admins' songs are released at once. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#before-everybody-hears-a-new-song)
- **The party's songs again on the guests' phones**: every guest hears the songs made at this party - once they played - again on their own phone, on the party's page (*Hear them again*); only this party's, and only songs made at it. A party begins when the guests' access is switched on, gets a new address, or the host starts a new one. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#guests-in-your-network)
- **Stars for every song, 0 to 5**: on a song's page in the player, the stars you give by ear (in halves; kept like your playlists, each user their own) and the measured ones - faded, a pre-fill until you give yours: how much of the lyrics as written is sung, how clean the harmony is (the notes against the chords, clashes a semitone apart, one key), how steady the beat, and what takes stars off (an ending cut off, silent holes, a song much shorter than asked, clipping, a loudness off its aim, the Song Sheet's warnings). *Details* lists every measure. Export Release measures each song, in about half a second, into its record (`quality`) and names its stars in its summary; a song exported before is measured once by the player. Both go into the song's own page (`<name>.html`): the measured stars, and the admin's once given. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#a-songs-page)
- **Tutorial 11 · YuE2 LoRAs**: the node, the two halves of YuE2 a LoRA shapes, a LoRA chosen with its strength, an instrumental made with it and what the node says after the run (`tools/tutorials/yue2-loras.mjs`). Tutorials 4, 8 and 9 are recorded again with what is new: the lyrics corrected before the release, the full lyrics to copy and the song's page, the studio started the way you start ComfyUI, the party's date and reactions.

### Changed

- **The visuals to choose from at a glance**, with a hundred of them: a search, the moods they suit, the groups folded with how many are ticked, each visual a small chip (what it shows on hovering); the video gallery in small windows (larger ones to switch) with a jump to each group. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#visuals)
- **Only visuals this device draws smoothly are offered**: without WebGL2 on the graphics card - no WebGL2, or WebGL drawn by the processor - Plenio Drop and Plenio Nova are not offered at all (the settings say why), and a song's or playlist's own visual of theirs falls back to the mode. Each Nova visual is timed once on the graphics card, in the background (which also compiles it ahead); one that would not stay smooth even at a small picture is not offered on that computer, the others get as many pixels as keep them smooth. While a visual plays, fewer pixels are drawn while frames come late, and a visual that still does not keep up is left out for the session (*Try again* brings it back).
- **The player starts ComfyUI the way you last started it**: every time ComfyUI loads Plenio, Plenio keeps its Python and its arguments (your `--models-directory`, your options); *Start the studio* and a song ordered while ComfyUI is not running use them when *Settings › The studio* has no arguments of its own - also for ComfyUI Desktop, whose folder has no Plenio in it. The settings show which arguments it uses. [Details](docs/user/concepts/player.md#the-studio-comfyui-started-by-the-player)
- **Party songs are the guests' songs only**: a song ordered on the party's page is saved in the day's party folder and named on the big screen; a song you or a user make in the player is saved like one made in ComfyUI (the template's folder and tags).
- **The big screen and the guests' phones say when a party song's party was** (*made at the party on 27 October 2026*), not *this party*.
- The sidebar names the songs' folder by its last two folders (the whole path in its tooltip; a click opens the folders in Settings).
- On a phone, Live Play's and Make a song's headings stand above their buttons, and the playlists come two in a row.
- **The README tells what Plenio does as a whole**: the pipeline from a brief to the player in one picture, ComfyUI and the player each on one page of pictures, short animated clips (the score editor with a MIDI keyboard, the LoRA node, the party's reactions), a section for the curious (the record, the Song API, the LoRA join, the tests) and the measured time of a song. The history of earlier versions is in this file.

### Fixed

- **A song ordered from the player stopped with "Load checkpoint: Value not in list"**, and *Every setting of the studio* listed only LM Studio's models, when the player had started ComfyUI without the models folder it is usually started with. Besides the start above, a missing model file is now named, with what to do (*ComfyUI does not find the model file …*), and a model field set to a file ComfyUI does not list says so.
- **Videos stopped now and then with "out of memory"**: every preview - one at each change in the video dialog - and every video kept the graphics memory of its Plenio Drop renderer until the browser collected it. They give it back at once now; a lost graphics context is made anew, the video's pieces on their way to the player are bounded, and a video that fails is tried once more by itself.
- **A console window popped up on Windows** - in Windows Terminal an error tab (*0x800700e8*) - when the player started ComfyUI, or ComfyUI started the player, with a venv's Python: its `python.exe` starts the real interpreter, which got a console of its own. Both start without a window now, and so do the programs they start in turn.
- **The copy buttons of a song's page** (the seeds, the record's path) did nothing in a player opened through *In your network*, whose plain HTTP has no clipboard API; they copy there too now.
- **Links to a section of the settings** (*Switch it on in Settings*, *How to start it*) opened the settings at the top when they were already open, or before the section had loaded.

### Security

- **What only this computer may ask is answered only on it.** ComfyUI has no login, and with `--listen` - or the player's *In your network* - everyone in the network reaches Plenio's routes in it. The System Check's report, which names the computer's folders and versions, and starting the Plenio Player now answer only a browser on the computer ComfyUI runs on; the System Check node still shows the report in the workflow. [Details](SECURITY.md#plenios-http-routes)
- **An error tells the network no more than that it happened**: a bug in one of Plenio's routes or the Song API is answered without its text, which can name a folder of the computer - ComfyUI's console keeps it. The player answers requests from the network alike, and names them no place of the computer: folders and files only by their names.
- **Only the admin and the admins' group take songs out of the library**: listeners, makers and guests keep their own playlists, not the songs, and *Remove…* shows only to whom may.
- **The player and Local LLM start only what they are meant to**: the studio's folder must be a ComfyUI folder (its `main.py` beside `folder_paths.py`, `execution.py` and `comfy`) and its remembered Python a Python; a `llama-server` named in the settings or in `PLENIO_LLAMA_SERVER` must be one, or another program is not started. An address of a page's own to look for ComfyUI at is taken only from this computer.

## 1.3.2 - 2026-10-27

**Lyrics you can correct in Enhance & Master, and instrumental covers that run again.** Enhance & Master gets an optional *Lyrics* block: the recording's words are heard, corrected on a Song Sheet and released as you wrote them. An instrumental cover no longer stops at the transcription, and the player's help plays the tutorials of 1.3.1.

### Added

- **Enhance & Master: correct the lyrics before the release.** An optional *Lyrics* block (bypassed until you switch it on) hears the recording's words and stops at a Song Sheet; the words fixed there go into the subtitles, the lyrics tag and the release record, timed to the singing. Before, the words went out as the ASR heard them, misheard ones included. [Details](docs/user/paths/enhance-master.md#correcting-the-lyrics)

### Fixed

- **An instrumental cover stopped at Transcribe Lyrics** (*The transcription found no vocal melody*) when the source had no singing, although the Cover Brief said *instrumental* (since 1.2.1). An instrumental cover now skips the transcription.
- **A Song Sheet waiting for review let an Export Release that reads only its report run**, with the unreviewed documents; the report now waits for the approval like the sheet's other outputs.
- With *copy from loaded file*, a Song Sheet's lyrics now win over the lyrics tag copied from the file.

### Changed

- **Help & tutorials in the player** plays the tutorials of 1.3.1: *8 · The Plenio Player* in its new recording, and the new *9 · Party songs* and *10 · Song API*. The player guide and the Song API guide link them too.
